01 // About the game

The night is watching you

PREY: THE ESCAPE is a first-person horror survival game for Android. Zara's brother and bunkmate, Duke, has vanished from the house. The town outside is sealed by locked gates and walked by things the game calls Ghouls and Zombies.

You get four points of health, sixteen inventory slots and a map that shows only what you have found. No quest markers. No hand-holding. You learn the streets by walking them, the locks by trying them, and the route out by surviving long enough to find it.

Everything runs on-device. No account, no connection, no ads — just you, the dark and the search.

02 // The story

EVERY NIGHTMARE
HAS A BEGINNING

The game opens in the dead of night, in a town where the streetlamps still burn and nobody answers. Zara is asleep. By the time she wakes, her brother Duke is gone.

I

The town that stopped breathing

“In the dead of night, morbid monsters and gruesome ghouls stir in the darkness. Rustles turn to screams and gentle gusts turn to shrieks.”

That is the game's own opening crawl, not marketing copy. It sets the rules before Zara has left her bed: the night belongs to something else, and the town knows it.

II

An empty bed

Zara sleeps restlessly, woken by yelps from the townfolk. When she gets up, Duke — her brother and bunkmate — is gone from the house. No note. No explanation. The game never says why he left; only that she will go after him.

The roads out are locked. Why the town is sealed is never explained. What the game shows is simpler and worse: every way out is a gate, and every gate wants a key.

III

The search across town

At the first locked gate Zara says it aloud: “Duke? Duke!? My brother must of ran off… maybe through that gate over there…” The backlot gate will not open. Arnold, a neighbour who knows her, insists he has not seen Duke. Further on, Johnny says the same.

The town keeps its answers behind locks — and the people who are still here only warn her to keep out of sight.

IV

What is hunting her

The build names two hostile types: the Ghoul and the Zombie. They patrol the streets, notice movement, investigate, chase and strike. Their origin, and why they are here, is never stated on screen.

They are not set dressing. A single Ghoul strike deals as much damage as Zara has health.

V

The way out

The escape is mechanical: keys carry codes, a two-slot electrical box powers a gate, and the exit is marked with a green light. Later, at the edge of the field, two generator keys and a multicoloured box stand between Zara and the East Gate.

Duke is found alive — hurt, calling her name. What lies beyond the East Gate is not established anywhere in the project. The story ends at the escape, not the explanation.

Case notes — what the game actually establishes

The place
A residential town after dark. In-game signage names the Backlot Gate, the Side Gate and the East Gate; the final map calls the outskirts “The field”.
Why she is trapped
Duke is missing, and every route out is locked. Why the town is sealed is undisclosed.
What she must find
Duke. A matching key for each gate, two generator keys for the electrical box, and a way to power the exit.
What is hunting
Ghouls and Zombies, patrolling on NavMesh routes. Their origin and purpose are undisclosed.
The escape
Open the powered gate and get out. What waits on the other side is undisclosed.
The rule
Scares come from the choices you make, not scripted jumps — the original design notes say so explicitly.

Survival notes — taken from the actual AI

  1. Walls are your only armour. Its vision needs an unobstructed line. Put a building between you and it, and the chase clock starts.
  2. Two seconds is the gap. Stay unseen for two seconds and it stops chasing. It waits four more, then returns to patrol.
  3. Speed is a loan. Zara sprints at 10 m/s against a 6–6.5 m/s chase, but the meter lasts eight seconds. Use it to reach a corner, not to win a footrace.
  4. Listen for the stab. A musical stab and the chase score mean you have been noticed. The silence afterwards is not safety — it is a four-second wait.
  5. The first hit can be the last. Zara has 4 health. A Ghoul strike deals 4; a Zombie deals 1. Bandages heal 1, med kits heal 2.
  6. Fighting is not the plan. The bat exists, but the game is built around avoidance and item management, not clearing rooms.

No abilities, weaknesses, lore or behaviour beyond the configured values above are presented as fact. The project does not explain what the creatures are.

Every key in the build carries a code, and examining it reads the engraving back to you. Locks want that exact code — not simply “a key”. The wrong one gets a line, not a door.
